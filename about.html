About us

Late Plate opened because the two of us kept finishing shifts at one in the morning with nowhere to eat. Everything within walking distance had closed hours earlier.

So we took a narrow room on Northwestern Avenue, put in a counter, eight stools and a griddle, and started cooking the food we wanted at the end of a long night. Breakfast at midnight. Burgers at two.

Most of the people who eat here work nights too, or study through them, and that suits us.
