Where do we park?

Street parking on Northwestern Avenue is free after 6 PM and all day Sunday. The garage on Wood Street is a three minute walk.

Can you cook around an allergy?

Tell your server before you order. The kitchen is small, so we can usually adapt a dish. We cannot promise a nut free kitchen.

How do we pay?

Card, phone or cash. There is no minimum on cards and no service charge.
