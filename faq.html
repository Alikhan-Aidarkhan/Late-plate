Where do we park?

Street parking on Northwestern Avenue is free after 6 PM and all day Sunday.

The garage on Wood Street is a three minute walk and stays open all night. We do not validate parking, but nobody has ever been ticketed on our block after dark.

Can you cook around an allergy?

Tell us when you book, or tell your server before you order. The kitchen is small, so we can usually adapt a dish rather than refuse it.

We cannot promise a nut free or gluten free kitchen. The same griddle cooks everything, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

We are a group of nine. Will you fit us?

Yes, with notice. The long table at the back seats ten and we hold it for groups that book at least a day ahead.

For anything over ten, write to us through the contact form and we will call you back.

How do we pay?

Card, phone, or cash. There is no minimum on cards.

We add no service charge. Tips go to the people who worked that night, split evenly between the kitchen and the floor.

Do you do takeaway?

Everything on the menu travels except the eggs, which do not survive the walk home.

Order at the counter and it will be ready in about fifteen minutes. We do not deliver and we are not on any of the delivery apps.
